An alarm with something on the line.

Snooze wins because nothing is at stake. Rise puts your SKR and your friends behind every morning.

1

Set your wake time

Drag the sun around the dial and pick a mission: find daylight, walk 30 steps, or scan the code by your kettle.

2

Make a pact

Friends lock SKR for each morning. Your wake window opens 30 minutes before your time and closes after a short grace period.

3

Clock in on chain

Finish the mission, sign once, and Solana's clock stamps your card. Missed mornings fill the pot, split by mornings kept.

Live from Solana devnet

These time cards are read straight from the Rise program as the page loads. Blue ink is on time, red is late, holes are missed mornings.
