A morning with Rise

Five minutes, start to stamp. Nothing here is a mock-up: these are screens from a OnePlus 10T on Solana devnet.

  1. Sunrise. Minutes before your wake time the alarm takes over the lock screen and the screen warms like a sunrise lamp. The sky is a real-time GPU shader moving from night to dawn.
  2. The stakes, out loud. At your wake time the tone climbs to full volume and the phone speaks: "Good morning, Rohan. It's 6:30. Aman and Priya are already up. 10 SKR is on the line."
  3. Prove you're up. Slide the sun to start your mission. The light sensor must read daylight, the step detector must count 30 steps, the camera must scan your wake-spot code, or on-device vision must see a morning scene.
  4. Clock in. One signature through Mobile Wallet Adapter. The program checks Solana's clock: only between 30 minutes before your wake time and the end of the grace period.
  5. The stamp. Your time is punched onto the pact's manila time card with a heavy haptic thump. Blue-black ink on time, red when late, a hole punched through for a missed morning.

How the pot works

Snooze wins because nothing is at stake. Rise puts your SKR and your friends behind every morning.

1

Set your wake time

Drag the sun around the dial and pick a mission. Rise Coach adjusts how hard the mission is to how likely you are to oversleep.

2

Make a pact

Friends lock SKR for each morning, 3 to 30 mornings long. Invite with a QR code or a link. Late joiners lock stakes only for the mornings left.

3

Clock in on chain

Finish the mission, sign once, and Solana's clock stamps your card. When the pact ends, kept mornings earn your stake back and the pot is split by mornings kept.

poteverything locked − stake × mornings kept by everyone
your payoutstake × mornings you kept + your share of the pot

Payouts always add up to exactly what was locked. If nobody wakes up at all, everyone is refunded. There is no admin key, no fee and no way for anyone, including us, to move SKR out of a pact.

Rise AI, on the device

Three models run on the phone. No camera frame, sensor trace or model ever leaves it.

Rise Coach

A logistic-regression model trained on the phone from the public time cards of every pact you're in, weighted toward your own mornings. It predicts tomorrow's chance you oversleep and sets the mission to Gentle, Normal or Tough, and says why.

Show the morning

A fourth mission: the camera feeds ML Kit's on-device image labeler, and the alarm stops only when it sees a morning scene, held for three seconds, above the coach's confidence threshold.

Proof anomaly scoring

Real sensors are noisy; spoofed ones come in exact steps or metronome-perfect strides. Every mission's trace is scored, and the confidence is written on chain with the clock-in, so a pact sees how each morning was proven.

Built for Seeker

Mobile Wallet Adapter 2.0
Every pact action is signed by the wallet: Seed Vault on Seeker, Phantom or Solflare elsewhere. Sign-and-send with a sign-only fallback, stale sessions re-authorised, every transaction simulated before the wallet opens. App identity verified through Digital Asset Links.
Seeker Genesis Token
Sign in with Solana, then the server checks the wallet for an SGT on mainnet. Mint authority, metadata pointer and group membership must all match. Verified owners get a badge; community pacts can keep seats one per device.
SKR, moving every morning
SKR is the stake and the reward of every pact. It moves between friends based on who gets up, so it is used daily instead of sitting still. No staking involved.
Phone hardware as proof
Ambient light sensor, step detector, camera with on-device scanning and labeling, exact alarms, lock-screen alarm, haptics and text-to-speech. None of this works in a browser, which is why Rise is native Kotlin and Compose.
